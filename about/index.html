About the channel

About Seriously, How?

Seriously, How? is a science documentary channel on YouTube. You may also see it written as Seriously How, SeriouslyHow, Seriously How TV or SeriouslyHowTV (our handle everywhere: @SeriouslyHowTV). It is all the same channel.

Subscribe on YouTube

What we make

Short documentaries and Shorts about the hidden systems of modern life and the planet they run on: what the internet really is and what happens when it stops, how a dead power grid is switched back on, how a volcano made the whole atmosphere ring like a bell, and how a crew flies into a Category 5 hurricane. Each film asks one plain question, the kind that makes you say "seriously, how?", and answers it with real data.

How we work

  • Sourced. Every number on screen is checked against a primary source (network operators, grid regulators, scientific papers, NOAA and other agency data, companies' own post-mortems) and the sources are listed in each video description.
  • Labelled. Real footage, real data, maps, models and reconstructions are labelled on screen, so you always know what you are looking at.
  • Honest about the tools. The narration is a synthetic voice. The research, scripts and fact-checks are done and reviewed by people.
  • Two languages. New films come with English and Turkish audio and subtitles.
