Privacy and cookies

Updated 20 November 2026

This site has no ads, no accounts and no forms. We use Google Analytics 4 to count visits and see which articles are useful, so we know what to make next.

What is collected

Only if you accept analytics cookies: pages viewed, time on page, scrolling, links clicked to leave the site, approximate location (country/city), device and browser type, and how you arrived (for example a search engine or YouTube). Google Analytics 4 does not log or store IP addresses. We cannot see who you are, and we do not combine this data with anything else.

If you decline, Google Analytics sets no cookies. With Google's Consent Mode, a browser that has not consented may still send a cookieless signal (no identifiers) that Google uses for aggregate counts.

Cookies

  • _ga, _ga_*: Google Analytics, set only after you accept, kept up to 14 months.
  • sh_consent (local storage, not sent anywhere): remembers your choice.

YouTube videos on this site load from youtube-nocookie.com, and only when you press play.

Change your choice

Use Cookie settings (also in the footer of every page) at any time. You can also clear your browser's site data.

Who processes the data

Google Ireland Limited / Google LLC, under the Google Analytics terms and Data Processing Terms. Data retention is set to 14 months. Questions: contact us through our YouTube channel, Seriously, How?.
