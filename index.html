How we work

Every number on screen is checked against a primary source and listed in the video description: network operators, regulators, standards bodies and the companies' own post-mortems. When something can't be verified, we leave it out or say it's an estimate. The narration is a synthetic voice; the research, scripts and fact-checks are done and reviewed by people.
